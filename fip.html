FIP

FIP = ((13×HR) + (3×(BB+HBP)) − (2×(SO+IFFB))) / IP + FIP constant
IFFB (infield fly balls) is optional — leave blank to skip it. Enter the FIP constant yourself (generally around 3.10, varies by year/league) — look it up, or derive it below.
FIP Constant = lgERA − (((13×lgHR) + (3×(lgBB+lgHBP)) − (2×lgK)) / lgIP)
League-wide season totals (AL, NL, or all of MLB — whichever you're matching).
